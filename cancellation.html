Cancellation Policy

This policy explains how you can cancel an order placed on the White Horse Clips online store, and what happens to your money. It forms part of our Store Terms of Sale and is read with our Returns and Refund Policy and Delivery Policy.

Effective 1 October 2026

  • Cancelling before your order is dispatched
  • Your 7-day cooling-off right
  • When the cooling-off right does not apply
  • If we cancel your order
  • How refunds are paid
  • Your other rights
  • Contact

01Cancelling before your order is dispatched

You may cancel an order, without giving a reason, until we have placed it with our supplier. Email info@whclips.co.za with your order number. If the order has not yet been placed with our supplier, we cancel it and refund everything you paid.

Once the order has been placed with our supplier, the 7-day right in section 2 applies.

02Your 7-day cooling-off right

Under section 44 of the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act, you may cancel an online purchase without giving a reason and without penalty, within 7 days of receiving the goods.

  • How to cancel. Email info@whclips.co.za with your order number and say that you are cancelling. You do not need to use a form.
  • Returning the goods. Send the goods back to us, with everything that came in the box, in the condition you received them. You may unpack and handle them as far as you need to decide whether to keep them. We will tell you the address and how to send them back.
  • Cost of returning. The only cost you carry is the direct cost of returning the goods to us.
  • Your refund. We refund the full price you paid, to the payment method you used, within 30 days of the date you cancelled, as the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act requires. We may hold the refund until the goods are back with us and checked against your order.

03When the cooling-off right does not apply

The right in section 2 does not apply to goods that by their nature cannot be returned, goods made to your own specification, and computer software whose seal you have broken. If we ever sell an item in one of these categories, we say so on its product page before you order.

04If we cancel your order

We may cancel an order if the product turns out to be unavailable, or if a price shown on the store was a clear error. We tell you by email before dispatch and refund you in full within 30 days. We never substitute a different product without your agreement.

05How refunds are paid

Refunds go back through PayFast to the card or account you paid with. How quickly the money shows on your statement depends on your bank, after we have issued the refund.

06Your other rights

Nothing in this policy limits any right you have under the Consumer Protection Act or the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act, including your right to return defective goods (see the Returns and Refund Policy).
