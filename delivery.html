Delivery Policy

This policy explains where we deliver and how long it takes. It forms part of our Store Terms of Sale.

01Delivery is included in the price

Delivery is included in the price, anywhere in South Africa. There is no separate delivery charge. The price you see is the total you pay.

Prices are in South African Rand. White Horse Clips (Pty) Ltd is not registered for VAT; no VAT is charged.

02Where we deliver

We deliver by courier to the address you give at checkout, anywhere in South Africa. We do not deliver outside South Africa. If your address is in an outlying area, delivery can take a day or two longer.

03How long delivery takes

  • Handling time: 1 to 2 working days (Monday to Friday) from the day your payment is confirmed. This is the time we and our suppliers take to prepare and dispatch your order.
  • Delivery time: 2 to 3 working days (Monday to Friday) after dispatch.
  • Order cut-off: orders can be paid for at any hour. Payments confirmed after 17:00 SAST, or on a weekend or public holiday, start counting on the next working day.

We do not hold stock. Your order is supplied by our suppliers after you pay. An order with several items may arrive in more than one parcel. These times are estimates, not guaranteed dates, but we fulfil every accepted order within 30 days unless we agree a later date with you. If we cannot, you may cancel and we refund you in full within 30 days.

04Tracking

We email you when your order is dispatched, with the name of the courier handling your parcel and your tracking or waybill reference.

05When your parcel arrives

Please check the parcel when it arrives, and tell us at info@whclips.co.za within 7 days of delivery if it is damaged or is not what you ordered, so that we can claim with our supplier in time. We put it right at no cost to you. This does not limit your rights under the Consumer Protection Act.

06If you are not there

The courier will try to deliver during working hours and will contact you if delivery fails. If a parcel cannot be delivered, we will contact you to arrange redelivery. If delivery failed because of an incorrect or incomplete address that you supplied, we may charge the cost of a second delivery; we tell you the cost before redelivering.

07Risk and ownership

The goods are at our risk until they are delivered to you. They become yours when delivered and paid for.
