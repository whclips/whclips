02 Why buy hardware from us

Official channel

Every item is sourced through official South African distribution channels. Genuine stock, full local manufacturer warranty, no grey imports and no parallel-market surprises when you need a warranty claim honoured.

Specified, not just shipped

We deploy this equipment in live plant environments. Compatibility checks, application guidance and honest advice about what you don't need come as part of the quote, not as a paid extra.

One point of supply

Hardware, integration and ongoing support from a single supplier on a single invoice — so there is no gap between who sold you the equipment and who is accountable for making it work.

03 What we supply

Displays & Workstations

Business and professional monitors, docking stations, desktop and mobile workstations for control rooms, engineering offices and site administration.

Networking

Managed and smart switches, wireless access points, SFP transceivers, DAC and fibre patch leads, and the structured cabling that ties a site together.

Power Protection

UPS systems, rack PDUs and surge protection for control rooms, server rooms and the edge cabinets that cannot afford an unplanned restart.

Storage & Memory

Solid-state drives, enterprise storage, memory upgrades and removable media for historians, edge nodes and engineering workstations.

Audio & Peripherals

Headsets, speakerphones and conferencing hardware, keyboards, mice and the desk-level equipment that distributed operations teams depend on.

Racks & Cabling

Patch panels, keystones, cable management, rack accessories and copper and fibre patch leads — specified to the cabinet, not guessed at.
